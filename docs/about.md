<div class="pf-about__text" markdown>

I am a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at Tufts University, minoring in AI Applications and earning the Fletcher School's Certificate in International Business. I research fused granular fabrication (FGF) for 3D printing soft robotics at the Nemitz Robotics Group, and in my free time am drawn to local AI, IoT security, and mesh and ad hoc networks. I also enjoy reading philosophical, sociological and political dialogue, playing chess, practicing piano, and physical activities like boxing, grappling, climbing and hiking. I love extending tech beyond the lab, devising how to make the most out of the least, and building with communicative clarity.

When permissible in design, I opt for locality: keeping data, computation and accountability as close to origin and use as possible. I believe systems should be simplified and decentralized whenever feasible to improve resilience, reduce external dependence and contain failure. A good system should carry information and assumptions openly, especially through version control, and be documented towards reproducibility. I aim for systems that are sustainable, efficient and secure, designing with modularity and minimized friction foremost to reduce cognitive load.

Some projects I'm planning for future learning are a XIAO ESP32 cluster for distributed wireless sensing and penetration testing across Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and sub-GHz bands; one or more drone builds for FPV flight, photography and environmental surveying; and further buildouts of my homelab towards local inference and network extensibility.

I am currently seeking internship opportunities for summer 2027, preferably working on systems related to robotics and autonomous operations, resilience and security, or guidance, navigation and control. [My inbox](mailto:samuel.linnington@tufts.edu) is always open for conversation and collaboration, professional or otherwise.
